Meet the Team

About us

We are Angel Rocketry, a team of students who design, build, and launch model rockets. This season we are competing in the American Rocketry Challenge (ARC), formerly known as TARC.

We are students from Emerald High School in Dublin, California, and we are passionate about engineering, science, and rocketry. Our team is dedicated to learning, experimenting, and pushing the boundaries of what we can achieve with our rockets.

The American Rocketry Challenge

ARC is the world's largest student rocket contest. Teams of middle and high school students design, build, and fly a model rocket to meet a precise set of performance goals. The goals change every year, so every team starts with a fresh engineering problem.

The contest is run by the Aerospace Industries Association and the National Association of Rocketry. Top teams from the national finals go on to represent the United States in international competition.

For the 2027 ARC, teams must design a rocket that carries two raw eggs to an exact altitude of 800 feet and safely returns them unbroken within 37 to 40 seconds.
